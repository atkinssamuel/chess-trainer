Chess Contender Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

Chess Contender ("we", "us") runs the chess-improvement web app at chesscontender.com. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short and plain.

What we collect

  • Account details. Your email address and authentication data, so you can sign in and we can save your work.
  • Your training data. The openings, repertoires, drill progress, endgame practice, and settings you create in the app.
  • Chess game data you connect. If you link a chess.com or Lichess account, we fetch and store your public games so you can review and analyze them.
  • Usage and device data. Basic analytics such as pages visited, actions taken, browser type, and approximate region, used to understand and improve the product.

How we use it

  • To provide the app: saving your repertoires, drills, and game history across devices.
  • To operate and improve features, fix bugs, and measure what works.
  • To measure the performance of our advertising, including via the Meta Pixel.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies and similar technologies for sign-in and analytics. We use the Meta Pixel to understand which ads lead to visits and sign-ups. You can control cookies in your browser settings and manage ad preferences through Meta's ad settings.

How we store and share it

Your data is stored with our infrastructure providers (including Supabase for the database and authentication). We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with service providers that help us run the app, or when required by law.

Your choices

  • You can view and edit your repertoires, drills, and linked accounts inside the app.
  • You can request deletion of your account and associated data by contacting us.
  • You can unlink a connected chess.com or Lichess account at any time.

Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your personal data except where we must keep records to meet legal obligations.

Children

Chess Contender is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy as the product changes. We will update the date at the top when we do.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email hello@chesscontender.com.
